macOS 11+ · Apple Silicon & Intel · free

JPEG-first speed

Culling never demosaics RAW. You see the camera JPEG (or the RAW's embedded preview), decoded ahead of your cursor — browsing thousands of photos is instant.

Keyboard-driven

Every action is one key, taught by a rail that dims once you've learned it. P X 1–5 K

Non-destructive

Ratings, flags, develop and crop live in an XMP sidecar next to the photo, in standard fields most editing tools read. Originals are never modified.

Develop, even on the RAW

White balance, exposure, tone curve, vibrance — as a live GPU pipeline. Press E to demosaic the RAW itself, one click matches the camera JPEG's look, and Match Look grades a whole burst against your reference frame.

See more before you decide

Compare V pits two frames head to head, Survey N winnows a burst down, Grid K keeps the whole working set in view while you mark.

One tool, any source

Folders, SD cards, network shares — or an Immich server and its albums, culled in place with ratings written back. Batch copy, move, export and upload treat a RAW+JPEG pair as one photo.

First launch

Builds aren't Apple-notarized yet, so macOS will refuse to open the app the first time — normal for any app from outside the App Store, and a one-time step per copy:

  1. Open the DMG and drag culler.app into Applications.
  2. Right-click the app → Open — or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
